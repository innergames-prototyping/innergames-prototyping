# Current Situation

InnerGames is a Dutch company that creates board games and workshop for educational purposes for a variety of clients in both the education and buisness sectors.

# Problem

When publishing a new game InnerGames takes on playtesters to ensure there are no issues with their works. However even with over a hundred individual testers trying dozens of times, more often than not issues still slip through the cracks. Additionally hiring large amounts of playtesters costs significant capital which could be saved.

# Solution

We are being comissioned to solve this by creating some kind of LLM based platform to not only digest and analyse their games but also run hundreds of simulations in order to catch bugs, expoits and other issues.
