## 2. Client feedback

**On the frontend**

- New users need some onboarding: where have I ended up, what is this, and what can I do with it?
- Keep it simple. The interface should explain itself, but a short "what is this" would help.
- People have little patience with new applications.
- Check whether the image placeholders and similar elements are clear enough.

**On showing what the (extraction) system is doing**

- Keep the process transparent. Make it clear, visually or in text, what is going on: what the system assumes something is, what is what, and what it is looking at. We need to work out how to show this to the user.
- Show progress, for example with a loading bar, and look into how best to do that.

**On AI and the source code**

- AI is a societal phenomenon, and this use is so niche that sending the material to Anthropic isn't an issue.
- Arien will think about how to protect the source code.
